Try it with both open

  1. Open both portals in separate tabs or windows, in the same browser.
  2. In the customer portal, type a part name and submit the request.
  3. In the admin portal, open that order and log “Request accepted”, then the next milestones.
  4. Switch back to the customer tab. The timeline updates on its own.
  5. Or turn on Auto-run in the admin portal and watch deliveries move.

The two pages share their data through your browser, so they stay in sync only within the same browser. Nothing is sent to a server.
